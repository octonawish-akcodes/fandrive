# Outreach drafts

Short messages to get real feedback from the people FanDrive is for. Send them yourself, from your own accounts. Only quote someone publicly (Devpost, README, video) after they say yes in writing.

**Who to contact**

- **US:** the donor recruitment, community engagement or blood drive coordinator team at a regional blood centre (for example Gulf Coast Regional Blood Center in Houston or Bloodworks Northwest in Seattle), or a university blood drive organiser.
- **India:** NSS unit coordinators, Rotaract clubs, college blood donation clubs and hospital blood bank social workers.

**What to ask for:** 10 minutes with the live demo and two sentences of honest feedback, plus permission to quote them by role ("Donor recruitment lead, regional blood centre") or by name.

---

## 1. Email to a US blood centre

**Subject:** A free tool for recruiting first-time donors under 25 (2 min to try)

Hi {first name},

I'm Abhishek, a developer building **FanDrive** for the Qloo Agentic Hackathon. It helps blood centres pick partners for youth drives using taste data rather than guesswork.

You type a city. An AI agent tests a dozen fandoms (artists, teams, shows, games and creators) against Qloo's audience data, then shows you:

- which fan bases skew young in your city;
- the neighbourhoods where those fans live;
- venues nearby that can host (schools, colleges, halls), plus poster and social copy ready to use.

For example, in Houston it ranks Kai Cenat's fan base (youth skew +0.88) far above legacy partners whose fans skew older.

**Try it (no sign-up):** https://fandrive-nu.vercel.app
**A recorded Houston run:** https://fandrive-nu.vercel.app/plan?example=houston

Would you spend 10 minutes with it and tell me, in a sentence or two, whether this would help your team plan a drive? Blunt answers are the most useful. If it's OK, I'd like to quote you, by role or by name, whichever you prefer, in my submission.

Thank you for the work you do.

Abhishek Kumar
abhishek22512@gmail.com

## 3. Follow-up, after someone tries it

Thanks so much for trying FanDrive! Three quick questions, and one-line answers are perfect:

1. Would you use this to choose a partner or theme for your next drive or camp? Why or why not?
2. What's the one thing it got wrong, or that's missing?
3. May I quote your answer in my hackathon submission? If so, as "{role, organisation}" or with your name?
