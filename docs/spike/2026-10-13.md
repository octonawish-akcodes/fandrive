## Fixes the live data forced

- **Rate limit.** The hackathon key allows 5 requests a second and 10,000 a month. The window carries the previous second, so a steady 4/s still drew 429s. The client now runs 3/s, pauses every caller for 1.1 s after a 429, and retries a 429 up to twice.
- **Venue genres.** Matching is whole-word (`bar` no longer matches `barbecue` or `espresso_bar`). Shooting ranges, cigar/vape/hookah, tattoo, liquor/beer stores, casinos and funeral homes are never suggested. Hotels and apartment blocks are skipped. Cafes are promote spots, not hosts. Schools, educational institutions, banquet halls, cultural centres, gurdwaras and synagogues can host.
- **Zone names** are unique. With a 4 km radius, two Miami zones were both "Wynwood".

## Known limits (shown under "Data limits")

1. **Badge.** Houston and Seattle show "medium" because their anchor heatmaps have fewer than 1000 cells. Cell count is not a pure measure of data richness: Mumbai (999) beats Houston (644).
2. **Local affinity saturates.** Every partner that passes Qloo's threshold scores 0.92–1.00, so `local` acts as a presence gate. City-specific signal comes from density (heatmap lift over the baseline brands).
3. **Youth-wide fandoms lead.** Fortnite, Stranger Things, MrBeast and Minecraft top most cities. Local icons (Bad Bunny, Travis Scott, Rockets, Kraken, Hawks) land in the top 5. Qloo's youngest bucket is "24 and younger", which includes people too young to donate.
4. **Atlanta venues.** Places near Hawks fan clusters are mostly restaurants: one host venue across three zones.
5. **India.** Arijit Singh (youth −0.16) and Virat Kohli (−0.28) skew older, with lower local affinity (0.92–0.93) than global fandoms, so Qloo's India youth signal is thinner.
6. **Quota.** An uncached city costs about 42 Qloo calls (about 250 for all six). The key had about 8,750 calls left on 2026-10-13, and the quota resets around 2026-11-02. Production relies on the Redis cache and recorded examples.
