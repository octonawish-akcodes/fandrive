# 2-minute demo video script

Optional (the rules don't require a video), but judges skim, and a short video carries the story. Record the screen at 1280×800 with your voice over it. Use the recorded runs so nothing waits on the network.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | Home page hero | "The Red Cross is in its second-ever national blood crisis, and donors under 30 are down about 39% since 2015. Blood banks already team up with fandoms, but they pick partners by gut feel." |
| 0:12–0:22 | `/method`, Metallica card | "Metallica's drives recruited just 16% first-timers. On Qloo, its fans skew 45 to 54. Famous isn't the same as young." |
| 0:22–0:40 | Click **Watch Houston**; the agent steps stream in | "FanDrive is an AI agent. It proposes fandoms, and Qloo adds what young Houstonians love that other cities don't. Then it scores each one: how young the fans are, how loved locally, how concentrated." |
| 0:40–0:55 | Report card: Jeremy Peña +0.98, Don Toliver, LOCAL TASTE chip | "It picks Astros star Jeremy Peña and Houston rapper Don Toliver, both local, both young-skewing." |
| 0:55–1:05 | Plain AI check panel | "Ask the same AI without Qloo, and it suggests Megan Thee Stallion, whose fans aren't especially young, and misses both." |
| 1:05–1:20 | Map and "Where to host" | "Qloo's heatmap shows where those fans live, and FanDrive finds venues nearby that can host: schools, halls, gyms. Bars only get flyers." |
| 1:20–1:32 | Poster and campaign kit | "It writes the poster, social posts and a pitch email, and never invents a number or implies an endorsement." |
| 1:32–1:45 | Receipts drawer, then `/method` eval cards | "Every Qloo request is a receipt you can re-run. Across 20 cities, 31% of plain-AI picks had fans who skew older. FanDrive's picks averaged +0.75 youth skew, versus +0.25." |
| 1:45–1:55 | Terminal: `claude mcp add … /api/mcp`, then an agent answer | "And any AI agent can use it through MCP." |
| 1:55–2:00 | Home page | "FanDrive: every fandom is a blood drive waiting to happen." |
